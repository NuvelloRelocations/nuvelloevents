# Staff and Management Guide — "Beyond Arrival" Registration Page

## What it is

A web page for our HR & Global Mobility Industry Roundtable, *Beyond Arrival*:

- **Date:** 12 January 2027
- **Time:** 6:00pm until 7:30pm
- **Venue:** Nuvello Head Office, DIP 1
- **Light refreshments**

We share the link on LinkedIn. Guests open it on their phone or computer and fill in a short form to secure their place. They don't need an account.

## How it works

1. A guest clicks the link in our LinkedIn post.
2. They enter their name, phone number, work email, company and job title. They can also tick a box if they want to hear from Nuvello Relocations in future.
3. They press **Submit**.
4. Within a few seconds:
   - the guest gets a **thank-you email** with the date, time and venue;
   - our events inbox gets an email with the guest's details.

The guest list is the set of emails in the events inbox.

## The review version

The current version is for management review only:

- A yellow banner at the top says **"Preview build — registrations are not sent"**.
- You can fill in and submit the form to see how it behaves. Nobody receives an email.
- Search engines won't list the page.

Once management approves, the team switches on the emails and publishes the page at a permanent web address.

## Before going live, we need

- [ ] Management approval of the page design and wording
- [ ] The events inbox that should receive registrations
- [ ] The link to our Privacy Policy
- [ ] Approval to use the external email service (guest details pass through it)

## If something looks wrong

| What you notice | What to do |
|---|---|
| The yellow "Preview build" banner is on the live page | Tell the AI and IT Team. Registrations are not being sent. |
| A guest says they never got a confirmation | Ask them to check spam or junk. If it isn't there, tell the AI and IT Team, and confirm the guest by email yourself. |
| The page won't open | Try another browser or mobile data, then contact the AI and IT Team. |
| A detail is wrong (time, venue) | Tell the AI and IT Team. The page and the confirmation email are updated together. |
| Spam registrations appear | Forward an example to the AI and IT Team. |
