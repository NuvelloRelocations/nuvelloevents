Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Nuvello Relocations uses the details you give us when you register for Beyond Arrival, the HR & Global Mobility Industry Roundtable on 12 January 2027.

What we collect

Your full name, phone number, work email, company and job title, and whether you agreed to receive other communications from us.

Why we use it

  • To manage your registration and contact you about this event.
  • To send you other communications, only if you ticked the box agreeing to this. You can change your mind at any time.

Who handles it

We use these services to run this page. They may process data outside the UAE under their own privacy policies.

  • Cloudflare runs the small service that receives the form, and its Turnstile check confirms that the sender is a person. It does not store your details.
  • Microsoft 365 delivers the details to our team and sends your confirmation email from our own mailbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this page, and Google Fonts supplies its lettering. Both receive technical details such as your IP address when you visit.

Our own code on this page does not set cookies. Cloudflare's Turnstile check may use its own to tell people from automated programs.

How long we keep it

Only for as long as we need it to run the event and, if you agreed, to contact you afterwards.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you. Email info@nuvellorealestate.ae or write to Office 304, AT Investment Park, Dubai Investment Park First, DIP, Dubai, UAE.
